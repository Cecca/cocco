## Signing in {#sign-in}

You sign in with your institutional email address. The sign-in page lists the
ways this server offers, and under them the addresses it accepts (for
instance `@unipd.it`, which also covers `@studenti.unipd.it`). Any other
address is turned away.

- **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with GitHub**: you sign in on Google's
  or GitHub's own page and come back here. There is no account to create:
  your first sign-in creates one. With GitHub, your institutional address has
  to be one of the verified addresses on your GitHub account.
- **Email address and password**, if the server offers it. The first time,
  open **I do not have an account yet**, type your address and choose a
  password of at least 12 characters. An address that has already been used
  to sign in can't be claimed this way. If that address is yours, sign in the
  way you did before, or ask a site administrator for a new password.

Your account belongs to your address, not to the way you signed in. Signing in
with Google one day and with a password the next takes you to the same
account, with the same courses and submissions.

A new account isn't on any course yet. See
[Joining a course](/docs/students#enrolling).

On a test server you may instead see a **Development sign-in** box, which
signs you in as whatever address you type, with no password. It exists for
trying cocco out and is never turned on for real use.

## Your profile {#profile}

**Profile**, in the menu under your picture at the top right, opens your
profile page. It shows your display name, your picture, the address you sign
in with, how many submissions you have made, and the courses you are on.

Your **Display name** is the name on your submissions, in your teachers'
standings and on every leaderboard. A new account starts with the part of
your address before the `@`. Change it in the box and press **Save**. It can be
up to 64 characters.

Your email address can't be changed: it is what you sign in with.

To leave a course, ask its staff. You can't remove yourself.

## Your picture {#avatar}

Every account starts with a generated picture, a pattern made from your
display name. It appears next to your name on leaderboards and in the menu.

To use a photo or drawing of your own, press **Change picture** and choose a
PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP file of up to 512 KB. It replaces the old picture as
soon as you pick it.

**Generate from my name** goes back to a generated picture, made from your
display name as it is now. Changing your name doesn't redraw the picture by
itself, so press it after a rename if you want them to match.

## Password {#password}

The **Password** section of your profile is there when this server allows
signing in with a password. With a password you can sign in with your address
alone, without Google or GitHub.

To set one, type it twice and press **Set password**. It must be at least 12
characters. To change it, you also type the current one, which stops anyone
who finds you signed in on a shared computer from changing it.
**Remove password** takes it off your account. Do that only if you have
another way to sign in.

Only a scrambled form of your password is stored, so nobody can read it back,
site administrators included. If you forget it, a site administrator can
issue you a new one. They see it once and pass it on to you. Sign in with it,
then set a password of your own straight away.
