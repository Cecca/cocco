# Pin HTMX

This repo loads a beta of htmx 4 (4.0.0-beta2), which swaps error responses into the page. CLAUDE.md assumes htmx doesn't, which was the behaviour of earlier versions. So across the whole app, a polled fragment that gets a 403 or 404 shows the server's raw JSON error where content should be.
